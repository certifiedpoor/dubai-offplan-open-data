## What the data is

- **Project register:** what developers have filed with the DLD for each project: escrow account status, construction progress, units and completion date.
- **Sales:** purchases recorded by the DLD. Prices are what buyers paid, not asking prices.
- Medians are calculated from individual sales. Areas need at least 20 recorded sales before a median is published.

Method: https://certifiedpoor.com/insights/how-we-read-the-dld-register
